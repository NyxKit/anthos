# Anthos logo exploration context

## Register

brand

This register applies to the logo exploration in this folder. Anthos itself is a product app.

## Users and purpose

The existing [product spec](../roadmap/product-spec.md) identifies plant hobbyists, people managing multiple plants, and technically curious owners as the primary audience. Anthos combines plant records, sensor readings, care history, and device management to help people understand their plants. Plant identity is independent of hardware identity.

## Brand context

The existing DESIGN.md describes a “Living Laboratory”, combining botanical data with technical instrumentation. The app already uses a purple circuit leaf. This experiment explores the four directions requested by the user: evolving that mark, playing with Anthos lettering, combining A with botanical forms, and using a plant icon. Calm, technical, and playful treatments are exploration variables, not an approved final personality.

## Principles and anti-references

- Make the connection to plants legible.
- Retain a route with a clear family resemblance to the current logo.
- Give the name and initial distinct routes of their own.
- Avoid reducing the whole product to moisture or to a hardware node, following the product spec.
- Compare every candidate on light and dark backgrounds before choosing.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Include single-color silhouettes and small-size specimens to support visual review. No additional accessibility standard or final branding choice has been established for this exploration.

## Sources and scope

Grounded in the user's exploration brief, docs/roadmap/product-spec.md, root DESIGN.md, app/src/shared/assets/theme.css, shared/src/logo.svg, and design/logo/. These notes are local to the experiments and do not establish new product policy.
