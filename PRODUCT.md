# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Home operators running Anthos on a local network, often on a NAS or small server, who need a dependable place to monitor nodes, sensor data, logs, and provisioning state. They want the system to feel trustworthy, low-friction, and easy to recover when something is offline or misconfigured.

## Product Purpose

Anthos helps people deploy, monitor, and manage a home monitoring system made of AtomS3 Lite nodes, a hub API, and a Vue/Tauri dashboard. Success means the operator can provision devices, understand health at a glance, and troubleshoot the system without leaving the local environment.

## Brand Personality

Technical, calm, precise.

The product should feel competent and intentional, with the confidence of a tool built for people who care about their setup working reliably.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards with interchangeable cards and empty enterprise polish.
- Overly playful consumer apps that hide technical detail behind friendly fluff.
- Loud neon “maker” interfaces that feel experimental instead of dependable.

## Design Principles

- Make system state legible first, decorative treatment second.
- Favor direct paths for monitoring, provisioning, and recovery.
- Keep technical language accurate and actionable.
- Show enough context to support troubleshooting without overwhelming the operator.
- Preserve a calm, confident tone even when the system is degraded.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA where practical.

Support reduced motion, clear contrast for dense telemetry, and readable states for users working in dim home environments or on small screens.
